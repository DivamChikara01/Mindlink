MindLink Sensor Ablation Experiment
========================================

Research question:
Which wearable sensor groups contribute most to MindLink stress-pattern detection?

Systems tested:
1. SVM only
2. Personalized baseline only
3. Combined SVM + baseline

Validation:
Outer subject-holdout testing. Thresholds and weights tuned on training subjects only.

Interpretation guidance:
- Highest stress F1 = best balanced stress detection.
- Highest stress recall = catches more stress windows.
- Fewer false stress alerts = less caregiver alert fatigue.
- Fewer missed stress windows = safer caregiver-support behavior.

Limitations:
- WESAD is lab data, not original MindLink wristband data.
- Personalized baseline uses WESAD baseline labels as simulated calibration.
- This is not emotion detection and not medical diagnosis.
